How to write a field story or update

A good entry is short, specific, and built around one moment or one number. Use this as the shape for anything posted to the site.

  • One headlineName the moment, not the topic. "A grandmother reads Luke," not "Literacy news."
  • Where and whenTown and country, and the date. It grounds the reader in a real place.
  • 80–150 wordsOne scene or one figure. Enough to picture it; short enough to finish.
  • One photo, if you have itLandscape, well lit, showing people or the work. Optional — never hold a story back for lack of one.
  • A captionOne line naming who or what is in the picture.
